# Advanced composition

Composite topics are **Beta**. Preserve member grains, shared dimensions, NULL/unmatched behavior, local filters and cross-fact ratios. Qualify their selected configuration separately; a diagram or tutorial is not native acceptance.

LOD requires explicit inner/outer grain and filter timing. Aggregate-awareness work requires eligibility, freshness and observed query-plan evidence; its exact contract remains further research in this package. Preserve opaque advanced constructs during later inventory, but block dependent edits until their semantics are resolved.

The present runtime does not generate or qualify these features. Expand one capability at a time using independent negative controls, not a blanket allowlist change.
